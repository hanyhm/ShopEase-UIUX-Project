# Agile Scrum Framework for ShopEase

## Sprint Structure
| Sprint | Duration | Focus | Deliverables |
|--------|----------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Week 1 | Research & Discovery | User interviews, competitive analysis |
| 2 | Week 2 | Define & Ideate | Personas, journey maps, problem statements |
| 3 | Week 3 | Wireframes | Low-fidelity layouts |
| 4 | Week 4 | Visual Design | High-fidelity UI, style guide |
| 5 | Week 5 | Prototype & Test | Interactive prototype, usability results |
| 6 | Week 6 | Review & Presentation | Iterations, final showcase |

## Team Roles
- **Product Owner:** Defines requirements and priorities
- **Scrum Master:** Facilitates Scrum process
- **UI/UX Designers:** All team members contribute to design

## Daily Stand-ups
- 15-minute daily meetings
- Progress updates
- Blockers identification
- Next steps planning

## Definition of Done
- Design reviewed by team
- User testing completed
- Feedback incorporated
- Documentation updated
